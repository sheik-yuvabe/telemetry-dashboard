# Student Prompting Guide

A **prompt** is the question or instruction you give an AI to tell it what help you need.

Use Codex as a learning partner. Work on one small task, try it yourself, and ask for help when needed.

## Course prompt template

Copy this and fill in the parts that apply:

```text
Week: <number>
Project: <project name>
Topics allowed this week: <topics>

My task: <one small thing I want to do>
I tried: <my attempt, or "just starting">
My problem: <what is confusing or not working>
Expected result: <what should happen>

Help I need: <a plan, a hint, an explanation, debugging, or a review>
Relevant code, file, or error: <add it here>

Use simple English and stay within this week's topics.
Help me with one step at a time and explain why it works.
```

## Remember

- Starting a project? Ask for a short plan first.
- Stuck? Share the exact problem and what you tried.
- Answer too difficult? Say: "Make it simpler. Give me one small step."
- After making changes, test them and ask for a review.
- Avoid asking Codex to build the whole project for you.

**Note:** This template is for this course, not every situation. Change it to match your task.
